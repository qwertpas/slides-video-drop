Drop videos into
Google Slides.

Drag an MP4, MOV, or WebM onto a slide. Slides Video Drop uploads it to your Google Drive and inserts a native video.


video.mp4
→
  1. Open a presentation and select a slide.
  2. Drop a video and connect the Google account used in that tab.
  3. The video uploads to a blue Slides Videos folder and appears on the selected slide.

Preview release. Public installation is not available yet. Google needs time to process uploads. Viewers need access to the video in Drive.
