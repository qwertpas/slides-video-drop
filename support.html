Help

Upload a video

Open a Google Slides presentation in your browser, select a slide, and drag an MP4, MOV, or WebM onto it. Connect the Google account used in that tab when prompted. Videos upload to a blue Slides Videos folder in that account's Drive.

Accounts

The extension uses the account shown in the current Slides tab. Open the extension menu to see it. If Slides does not expose an account email, use Choose account. Each account grants access separately. Workspace administrators may restrict third-party apps.

Upload or insertion stopped

Open the extension menu. If the file finished uploading, use Retry insert to insert it without uploading again. Otherwise, use Cancel upload and drop the file again. Uploaded Drive files remain available after cancellation.

Video will not play

Google may still be processing it. Use Open video to check it in Drive. For format errors, export as MP4 with H.264 video and AAC audio. Check Drive sharing permissions when presenting to other people.

Folder reuse

The extension reuses its Slides Videos folder and recreates it if deleted. Its limited Drive permission cannot discover an unrelated folder created outside the extension, even if the name matches.
