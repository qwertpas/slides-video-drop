Privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026 · Developer: Christopher Xu

Slides Video Drop uploads videos you choose to your Google Drive and inserts them into the Google Slides presentation you are editing. It has no developer-operated server, analytics, advertising, or tracking.

Data used

The extension reads the signed-in account email displayed in Slides and verifies that email with Google. It handles the chosen video's name, type, dimensions and bytes, the current presentation and slide identifiers, and the Google file and folder identifiers needed to complete your upload. It reads slide identifiers and page sizes to place the video.

Google permissions

Google Slides access lets the extension insert videos into an existing presentation selected by you. Google Drive file access lets it create and manage its upload folder and video files. Email access lets it match authorization to the account used in the current Slides tab. Although the Slides permission allows broader access, the extension uses it only for your requested video insertions and does not read slide text or images.

Storage and retention

Your selected account email is saved in the browser's extension storage until you disconnect that account or uninstall the extension. Access tokens, connected-account emails, and pending upload details, including the resumable upload URL, are kept in extension session storage, which is cleared when the browser session ends. Disconnect removes that account's locally cached authorization. Cancelling an upload removes its pending task.

Uploaded videos and the Slides Videos folder remain in your Google Drive until you delete them. Cancelling, disconnecting, or uninstalling does not delete Drive files or presentation elements. Video bytes are read from files you explicitly drop and sent directly to Google; the extension does not retain a separate local copy.

Sharing and Limited Use

Video data and the identifiers needed for insertion are transmitted directly to Google over HTTPS. The developer does not receive your videos, presentations, account tokens, or account email through the extension. The extension does not sell data, share it with advertisers or data brokers, use it to train AI models, or allow human access to your Google data.

Slides Video Drop's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Sharing a presentation does not automatically share its videos; you control video permissions in Google Drive.

Your controls

Use Disconnect in the extension menu to remove cached access for an account. To revoke Google's authorization, remove Slides Video Drop from your Google account connections. Delete videos and folders in Google Drive and remove inserted video elements in Slides when no longer needed. Uninstalling removes the extension's local data.

This website and support

This static website is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no analytics, cookies, external fonts, or embedded tracking. GitHub may process ordinary request information under its privacy statement. If you email support, the developer receives the message and information you choose to include and uses it to respond. Do not send passwords or access tokens.
